### What is physically placed

- Two rigid building rectangles inside an equal-area polygon split. Buildings
  stay rectangular on skewed lots; a bounding rectangle alone cannot pass a site.
- Both 5-ft and 3-ft side options. The 3-ft option requires rated side walls and
  controlled openings, with 3-ft clear pedestrian paths beside the buildings.
- Four 9-by-19-ft parking reservations per duplex: two tandem pairs, one per
  dwelling. Eight spaces across the project. Houston permits tandem spaces
  reserved for the same dwelling under 26-582(c).
- An 18-ft front driveway on children at least 44 ft wide, retaining 20 ft of
  continuous unobstructed curb; a 12-ft entrance flaring to the parking pad on
  33-to-under-44-ft children, for sites no larger than 15,000 sq ft.
- A 3-ft pedestrian connection beside the parking, 3-ft front crosswalk, 4-ft
  entry reservation, and 5-ft rear reserve. The stall sizes, six-foot flare depth,
  entry and rear reservations are chosen modeling dimensions. The parking and
  entry arrangement places the building 46 ft back, or 52 ft with the narrow flare.

These arrangements produce approximately 92-ft combined frontage for the broad
footprint with 3-ft sides, or 72 ft for the narrow footprint. They are outputs
of the chosen layouts, not a new universal frontage filter. Rectangular
100-by-100, 88-by-114, 80-by-125 and 74-by-150 sites fit; 50-by-200 and 70-by-150
sites do not fit either tested front-access arrangement.

**3531 Wentworth fails both working layouts.** Its 74-ft street span masks skewed
side boundaries: a rigid building with its access reservations does not fit both
equal-area halves. A rectangular 74-ft-wide parcel can fit the narrow layout.

Corner, through-street and major-road sites remain in their own map group. They require
street-side building lines and a different driveway arrangement; a front-only
placement does not count as a completed access layout. No front-only building or
parking overlay is shown on that group. Major-road frontage is flagged from the
city road class because 42-188(b) requires a shared/flag/turnaround access
arrangement, rather than the modeled two direct driveways. The engine also rejects apparent street
access when the line to the road crosses an intervening parcel.

The former 66-ft/33-ft rule came from Chapter 42's narrow-lot access provision.
It did not test buildings and no longer grants a product-fit pass.

## What Houston's rules actually distinguish

The following access rules concern property being subdivided. Existing legal lots
retain a separate plat/access review. Chapter 42's single-family residential
category includes a duplex on one lot.

| Item | Rule and acquisition consequence |
|---|---|
| City minimum lot area with sewer | Generally 3,500 sq ft under 42-181; the buyer's approximately 5,000-sq-ft child parcels exceed that general minimum. Special minimum lot sizes are separate recorded geographic controls. |
| General minimum lot width | 20 ft under 42-185, with a 15-ft option tied to shared access and averaging standards. This does not set the width of a ten-bedroom duplex building. |
| Mid-block lot at least 44 ft wide | 42-188(g)(1) requires at least 20 ft of unobstructed curb space and at least one-third of the street-facing structure facade to be occupiable space. Two equal lots enter this width category at 88 ft combined frontage. |
| Mid-block lot 33 to under 44 ft wide | For property totaling no more than 15,000 sq ft, 42-188(g)(2)(b) allows a direct-driveway route with a maximum 12-ft driveway width. Two equal lots enter this category at 66 ft combined frontage. Larger developments use the shared-access route. |
| Newly created lots below 33 ft | Test shared driveway, flag access, alley or another qualifying access arrangement. The limited existing-property exception in 42-188.5 has date, school-distance and Complete Community conditions; do not assume it applies. |
| Maintained alley | Subdividing property abutting an available maintained alley takes vehicle access from the alley. |
| Corner site | Test access from the side street under 42-188(f); retain each street frontage separately. |
| Front building line | Local-street baseline is 20 ft. Performance standards provide 10-ft and, with qualifying access/entry arrangements, 5-ft alternatives. A reduced building line does not itself provide parking or maneuvering area. |
| Parking for the duplex pathway | Generally two off-street spaces per dwelling unit: four per duplex, eight for this project. A unit at or below 1,500 sq ft needs one. The no-parking exception additionally limits a dwelling to 1,000 sq ft and imposes frontage/facade/access conditions. Proximity to an ordinary bus stop alone is not a parking exemption. |

Sources: [adopted Ordinance 2023-801, exhibit pages 17–18 and 21–29](https://www.houstontx.gov/planning/Livable%20Places/docs_pdfs/Signed_Adopted_Ordinances_-_C42_and_C26.pdf),
[city platting notes, single-family examples](https://www.houstontx.gov/planning/DevelopRegs/docs_pdfs/ORD_NOTE.pdf),
[2025 city parking review presentation](https://www.houstonpermittingcenter.org/sites/g/files/nwywnm431/files/2025-11/Pro%20-%20Session%202%20-%20Rm%20JK%20-%20FAQ%20-%20Site%20Plan%20Review%20101.pdf).

## Tight gaps between buildings

Houston does not impose a universal ten-foot separation between these duplexes.
Residential Code Table R302.1 evaluates the distance from an exterior wall to the
lot line. For the ordinary nonsprinklered table:

- At 5 ft or more, an unrated exterior wall and unlimited openings are allowed.
- At 3 ft to under 5 ft, the wall is rated and openings are limited to 25% of wall
  area. Houston applies that opening calculation by floor level.
- Below 3 ft, openings are prohibited. Houston's Code Word 2021-R04 also requires
  a maintenance agreement with the adjoining owner for construction this close.
- Roof projections cannot extend within 2 ft of the property line. Eaves can make
  the visible gap smaller than the wall-to-wall distance.
- The sprinklered table changes wall/opening allowances at 3 ft. It does not
  justify assuming arbitrary windows in a sub-3-ft side wall.

Two buildings each 3 ft from their shared line have a 6-ft wall-to-wall gap.
Closer walls are possible with the appropriate design. Each bedroom's emergency
escape opening still needs an eligible exterior location and a path to the public
way. Houston's R310.1 specifies a 36-inch yard/court, with projections not reducing
clearance below 32 inches through the first 80 inches of height. A blank side wall
therefore changes bedroom placement, not just the wall specification.

Sources: [Houston 2021 code comparison, pages 68 and 81](https://www.houstonpermittingcenter.org/media/9571/download?inline=),
[Houston Code Words, 2021-R04/R05/R08, PDF pages 48, 49 and 52](https://www.houstonpermittingcenter.org/media/8551/download?inline=).

## How to calculate fit

For an equal side-by-side split:

```text
child frontage = usable frontage on that street / 2
building width available = child frontage - left reservation - right reservation
building depth available = child depth - front reservation - rear reservation
```

Reservations include the actual wall clearance, easements, pedestrian route,
driveway/parking arrangement and applicable building lines. Keep the selected
building's width, depth, stories and window layout as explicit inputs.

The arithmetic below uses 3-ft side clearances to illustrate why width and depth
must be evaluated together. The last column is available building width, not a
preselected floor plan.

| Rectangular 10,000-sq-ft site | Each equal parcel | Building width after 3 ft on each side |
|---|---|---|
| 100 × 100 ft | 50 × 100 ft | 44 ft |
| 90 × 111.1 ft | 45 × 111.1 ft | 39 ft |
| 80 × 125 ft | 40 × 125 ft | 34 ft |
| 70 × 142.9 ft | 35 × 142.9 ft | 29 ft |
| 60 × 166.7 ft | 30 × 166.7 ft | 24 ft, plus an access arrangement to solve |

A published two-family plan, Architectural Designs 72964DA, has a 40 × 56-ft
building envelope and three stories, with each unit described as 5 bed/5 bath.
It demonstrates one specific design dimension, not the minimum for this buyer's
two-story product. The publisher's total-bath field disagrees with its unit
descriptions, so it is not used as a calibration plan.
[Publisher's dimensions](https://www.architecturaldesigns.com/house-plans/two-family-house-plan-for-a-narrow-city-lot-72964da).

Do not calibrate the buyer's ten-bedroom duplex from the study's entire
co-living set: the 31-ft Real Street examples are six-bedroom buildings, several
ten-bedroom records are single-family classifications, and Anita's publicly
advertised units are 3 bed/2.5 bath. Those establish alternative products, not the
Drew floor plan.

## Campaign ranking

The first completed ranking is in
[campaign-pockets-2026-10-26.md](campaign-pockets-2026-10-26.md), covering all
21,722 exported sites and 410 campaign squares. Oak Acres, Houston Harbor/Liberty
Heights and Forest Hill lead the recommended field sequence.

Rank the potential supply in separate lanes: two frontage lots, narrow frontage
lots, corner/through-lot opportunities, and shared-access opportunities. Keep the
measurement queue visible. Large deep parcels may support front/rear parcels;
do not count them as ordinary side-by-side projects or delete them entirely.

Before counting same-owner assemblies as immediately controllable, require
shared-edge contiguity and consistent owner evidence. The transferred pipeline
groups small parcels within 1 ft and sometimes uses confidential-owner mailing
addresses as keys. It also omits combinations that add a small parcel to an
already-10,000-sq-ft account. Preserve these as explicit inventory improvements.

For concentrated campaigns, report unique individual owners, estates and trusts
separately, and distinguish owners mailing to the subject site, elsewhere in the
pocket, elsewhere in Houston, and outside Houston. A Houston mailing address
alone does not mean a neighborhood campaign reaches that owner at home.
